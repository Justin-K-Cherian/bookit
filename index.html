About BookIt

One Platform.
Every Campus Space.

BookIt is a centralized web-based platform designed to simplify the reservation and management of college venues and sports facilities at MBITS.

It enables students and staff to check facility availability, book time slots, manage their reservations, and submit feedback through an intuitive interface. Administrators can monitor bookings, manage requests, and analyze facility usage to ensure efficient and transparent resource management across the campus.
